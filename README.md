## Project Overview
EVORA is a Family Safety system that connects personal safety, child safety, elderly care, emergency response, and family connection in one place. It helps families stay safe, supported, and connected while respecting privacy, dignity, and independence.

---

## Project Objectives
- Connect different family safety needs in one integrated system.
- Provide appropriate support for children, adults, older adults, and people with additional care needs.
- Enable clear communication, care coordination, and emergency response.
- Balance safety and support with privacy, dignity, and independence.

---

## Project Scope
- Personal Safety
- Child Safety
- Elderly & Care
- Emergency Response
- Family Connection

> **Final Deliverable:** A complete Family Safety ecosystem presented through UX research, personas, empathy maps, affinity mapping, user journeys, user flows, information architecture, key features, and UI/UX design.
